# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

DaemonCore Academy: Intro to Go does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, or online tracking.

Course progress, bookmarks, streak information, and personal lesson notes are stored locally on the user's device. A user may export that information to a JSON backup, import a backup they choose, or erase it with the in-app reset control. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information depending on Windows settings.

The app's course content and learning tools work offline. It does not send code entered in a lesson to DaemonCore or any third party.

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the support contact on the product's Microsoft Store listing.
